Reminder:

    manual step — see docs/runbooks/install.md.
  * All Secrets are referenced by name only; create them BEFORE the pods
    can start (see install.md "Secrets") unless certManager.enabled covers
    the internal mTLS Secrets.
  * `helm uninstall` keeps the managed namespaces, user PVCs and CRDs.
